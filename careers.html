I own six vacation rental homes, all inside the Towamensing Trails community in Albrightsville — no driving all over the Poconos, every house just minutes from the last. I'm hiring one full-time person whose main job is turnover cleaning, but this is more than a cleaning job — it also covers day-to-day caretaking of the homes and grounds. Why this beats cleaning gigs

  • Steady, full-time work. Right around 40 hours most weeks, year-round — summer and winter. No chasing clients, no dead weeks, no wondering what this month will pay.
  • Legit W2 employment. Taxes withheld, workers' comp coverage, steady weekly paycheck.
  • Time to do it right. You'll never be rushed through a clean — I'd rather you spend unhurried hours making a house spotless than race through it.
  • A schedule you can count on. 4 or 5 days depending on the week's checkouts, published in advance, with two to three days off every week.

The Work

Turnover Cleaning

  • Full clean between guests — kitchens, baths, floors, dusting, beds
  • Laundry & linens: washing, changing, keeping par stock
  • Staging each home so it looks like the listing photos
  • Restocking supplies and flagging anything that needs attention
  • Logging each completed clean in our simple phone dashboard

Property & Grounds Caretaking

No experience needed here — I'll train you on all of this.

  • Running the community office and trash facility
  • General upkeep of the exterior and grounds — walking each property, inspecting for and picking up trash, keeping the outside tidy
  • Spotting needed repairs and handling small fixes on the spot (batteries, a loose hinge, minor stuff)
  • Shoveling walkways and steps in winter
  • Light errands as needed
