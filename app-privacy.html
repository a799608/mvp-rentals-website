MVP Rentals App: Privacy Policy

Effective date: [DATE OF LAUNCH]

Who we are

The MVP Rentals app is published by William Morris, doing business as MVP Rentals. Contact: wm.m.morris@gmail.com or (610) 621-0769. This policy covers the MVP Rentals app for iPhone and Android. Our website and booking requests are covered by our website privacy policy.

What the app collects

  • Phone number: only if you sign in. We use it to text you a one-time sign-in code and to match you to your reservation.
  • Your requests and messages: food or grocery requests, questions you send to us, places you suggest, and ratings you give.
  • Notification token: only if you turn on notifications, so we can send trip reminders and occasional offers.

The app does not collect your location, contacts, photos, or payment information, and it does not contain advertising or third-party tracking. You can browse homes, open dates, and the area guide without signing in.

How we use it

  • To show your stay details, and your door code and WiFi from check-in day through checkout.
  • To handle your food and grocery requests: we text you a total, and once you approve and pay, we place the order with a delivery service on your behalf.
  • To answer your questions and improve what we recommend to guests.
  • To send notifications you have turned on. You can turn them off at any time in your phone's settings.

Text messages

  • When you sign in we text a one-time code to the number you enter. Msg & data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.
  • No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Who we share data with

  • Twilio Inc. sends our text messages.
  • Google hosts the spreadsheet where requests and sign-in records are stored.
  • Expo / Apple / Google deliver push notifications if you turn them on.
  • Delivery services and restaurants (for example Uber Eats or a local pizzeria) receive the order details and delivery address needed to fill a request you approved.
  • Government agencies, only when required by law.

We do not sell or rent your information.

How long we keep it

Sign-in sessions expire after 120 days. Requests and ratings are kept while your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete your phone number, sessions, notification token, requests, and ratings from the app's records. Reservation records themselves are kept for tax and accounting purposes as described in our website privacy policy.

Deleting your account

In the app: My stay > Settings & privacy > Delete my account. Or use our account deletion page.

Security

Sign-in codes expire after 10 minutes and are limited to a few tries. Session tokens are stored in your phone's secure storage and kept only in hashed form on our side. Door codes are shown only to the guest on the reservation, and only during the stay.

Children

The app is for adults booking or staying at our homes. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the current version.
